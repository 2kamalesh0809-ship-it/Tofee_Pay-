About PROMPTIX PAY

Our Identity

PROMPTIX PAY is the specialized, internal billing and customer management portal for our organization. We designed this platform to streamline how we manage our service vouchers, customer groups, and internal billing operations.

Not a Payment Gateway

It is important to note that PROMPTIX PAY is **not a payment aggregator, payment gateway, or financial intermediary**. We do not provide payment infrastructure for third-party businesses or other individuals.

This platform is a proprietary tool used exclusively by our organization to collect payments directly from our customers for the services we provide. Every transaction processed through this portal is a direct merchant-to-customer interaction.

Our Purpose

Our mission is to provide a seamless and professional billing experience for our organization and its customers. By centralizing our service management and billing in one secure portal, we ensure absolute transparency and efficiency in our internal operations.

Support & Reliability

We are committed to providing exceptional support to our customers. Our internal team actively monitors all billing operations to ensure every transaction is secure and every service voucher is accurately tracked.
